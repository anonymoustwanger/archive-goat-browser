FAQ

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

New apps without a paid code-signing certificate get this warning. Click More info, then Run anyway.

How do I run the Linux version?

Download the .AppImage, right-click → Properties → allow executing (or chmod +x), then double-click it.

Does Goat make me anonymous?

It stops ads, trackers and fingerprinting. Sites still see your IP unless you turn on Tor or use a VPN.

Where is my data?

Only on your computer. No account, no sync. Updating keeps your tabs, cookies and settings.
